# Third-party material

The vendored verl tree at `exp1/verl` retains its Apache-2.0 license, upstream README, package metadata and file-level copyright notices. Public dependency URLs, maintainer addresses, citations and acknowledgments identify their respective upstream projects. They are not MAS-Gate author metadata and have not been stripped.

Additional copied algorithms and adapters retain any existing file-level notices. The root Apache-2.0 grant applies to project-owned contributions and does not override third-party terms. Datasets, model weights and manuscripts are not bundled in this anonymous code release. Synthetic test inputs and blank configuration templates are included.
